Late Blight of Potato (Pakistan, December 2012)

Late blight can be controlled in different ways, before and after disease appearance. • Plant good quality, certified, seed tubers. Preferably save your own healthy seed tubers. • Cultivate resistant varieties • Locate your garden on well-drained soil where it will receive morning sun • Apply water around the base of plants and avoid wetting foliage in the early evening • Do not cultivate potato in infected fields for at least two years. Always practice appropriate crop rotation. • Remove blighted tubers before putting in store • Use fungicidal sprays on the appearance of initial symptoms, e.g. Dithane M-45 or chlorothalonil (Bravo) 500 SC, 2.5 kg/1 000 litres of water/hectare or Metiram (Polyram) 70 WG 1.8kg/ha, Propineb (Antracol) 70 WG 1.8kg/ha, Cymoxanil+ mancozeb, (Curzate M) 2.5kg/ha. Spraying should be repeated at 10-12 day intervals. Do not wash potatoes that are to be stored.
