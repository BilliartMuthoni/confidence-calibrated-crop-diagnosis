Late Blight of Tomatoes (Pakistan, September 2013)

• Use clean seed and resistant varieties (e.g. Naqeeb available from AARI, FSD) • Remove and destroy infected leaves and fruits (be sure to wash your hands afterwards) during the crop season and after harvest • Crop rotation for at least four years with crops like maize, millet, sunflower or pumpkin • Avoid heavy use of nitrogen fertilizer to avoid disease • Keep separate fields for early and late crops to slow down the spread of disease • Chlorothalonil 250g/acre during wet weather conditions • Copper oxychloride 3-4g/litre of water when leaves are wet due to dew. Apply at 7-10 day intervals with maximum 3-4 applications per season. • Cymoxanil 60DF @ 250g/100L water/acre, metalaxyl + mancozeb 68WP @ 250g/100L water/acre and dimethomorph 50WP @ 250g/100L water/acre apply at 10-14 day intervals with maximum 3 applications per season in wet weather condition.
