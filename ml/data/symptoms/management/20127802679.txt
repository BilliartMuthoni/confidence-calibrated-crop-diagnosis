• Keep the crop dry by planting crops wide apart to allow free flow of air between plants. For example, plant tomato at least 60-90 centimetres (two to three feet) apart. • Keep the plants healthy during the first 6 weeks after transplanting by using recommended fertilisers. Additionally, you can buy a fungicide at the agro-shop. Apply the fungicide at the rate shown on the label. • Prune overgrown plants to keep humidity low and to let in the air. Remove diseased plant parts from the field and dispose. Do NOT plough debris into soil! • Mulch around plants to reduce soil-water splash and help keep spores off healthy leaves. • Do not replant these types of crops (peppers or tomatoes) in the same field for at least two to three years. This is called ‘ crop rotation ’. • Apply a copper-based fungicide to infected plants at the prescribed rate on the label every 14 to 21 days or as recommended.
