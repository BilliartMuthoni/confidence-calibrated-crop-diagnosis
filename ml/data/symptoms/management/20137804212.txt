Early blight on Tomato (Kenya, December 2012)

Non-chemical control: • Use certified disease free seeds and tolerant varieties. • Practice crop rotation with non-host plants and plants that do not belong to the tomato family. • Avoid plant injury during transplanting. • Ensure a strong plant growth so that plants can escape attack of the fungus. • Plants should be staked and pruned correctly to allow good air flow and avoid disease spread. • Avoid planting tomatoes next to related crops like eggplant, pepper and potatoes. • Remove and destroy the old plants and weeds. Chemical control: • Some of the effective chemicals available include Mancozeb, Azoxystrobin, copper oxychloride , Cymoxanil + Famoxadone, Dimethomorph + Mancozeb.
