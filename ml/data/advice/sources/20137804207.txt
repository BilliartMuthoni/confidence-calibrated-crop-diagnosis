FACTSHEETS FOR FARMERS www.plantwise.org Created in Kenya , December 2012 Bacterial Spot on tomato Recognize the problem Bacterial spot disease is also known as bacterial scab, stem canker or leaf spot. Infection appears on leaves as water soaked areas. Leaf spots turn from yellow to dark brown. Affected flowers drop off. Fruits show a water soaked circle which soon disappears. Afterwards the spots become brown, slightly raised and scabby and often crack. Sometimes the spots are surrounded by a yellow ring. Background The disease is found in the seed and may affect both seedlings and mature plants. Disease is spread in the farm when working during wet weather conditions. It can also spread if irrigation is done late in the day and especially when using overhead irrigation. Management Non-chemical control • Plant certified disease-free seeds. • Practice crop rotation with crops not related to tomatoes (e.g. not pepper, brinjals or potatoes) • Avoid working on wet fields since the disease spreads faster. • Use flood or furrow irrigation where possible and in case of overhead irrigation, irrigate at a time when the leaves will dry quickly. Chemical control • Use a copper-mancozeb mixture to treat the bacteria.
