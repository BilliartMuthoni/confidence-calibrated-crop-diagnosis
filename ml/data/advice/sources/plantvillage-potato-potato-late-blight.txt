Cause Oomycete Comments The pathogen can survive for several months to years in the soil; emergence of disease favored by moist, cool conditions; major cause of disease spread is infected tubers Management Control depends on a multifaceted approach with importance of certain practices changing based on geographic location: destroy infected tubers; destroy any volunteer plants; application of appropriate fungicide to potato hills at emergence; time watering to reduce periods of leaf wetness e.g. water early to allow plant to dry off during the day; plant resistant varieties; apply appropriate protective fungicide if disease is forecast in area
