Cause Fungus Comments The disease mainly spread through rain splash and wind. Management Follow proper tillage to reduce fungus inoculum from crop debris. Follow crop rotation with non host crop. Grow available resistant varieties. In severe case of disease incidence apply suitable fungicide.
