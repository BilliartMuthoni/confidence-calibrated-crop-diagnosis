Cause Fungus Comments Disease emergence favors cycles of wet and dry conditions with periods of high humidity and leaf wetness Management Application of appropriate protective fungicide can reduce severity of foliar symptoms; reduce stress to plants by fertilizing and watering adequately; plant late varieties which are less susceptible to disease; store tubers in cool environment
