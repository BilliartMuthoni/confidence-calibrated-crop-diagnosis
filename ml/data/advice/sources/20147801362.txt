FACTSHEETS FOR FARMERS www.plantwise.org Created in Pakistan , December 2012 Late Blight of Potato Recognize the problem Late blight of potatoes is caused by a fungus-like organism. The first symptoms of late blight in the field are small, light to dark green, wet spots on the leaves. During cool, moist weather, these spots expand rapidly into large, dark brown/black spots. These become dry and destroy the leaf. The disease infects leaves, stems and tubers and can cause devastating crop losses. Entire foliage can become blighted and killed within just a few days. Infected tubers have dark, sunken areas on the surface, which may extend to cover the whole potato, giving a dry firm rot. Cutting the potatoes in half will show a patch of coloured rot. Severely affected plants have a distinctive odour because of the rotting leaves and tubers. Seemingly healthy tubers of infected crops may rot later when in store. Background The fungus can only survive in living infected potato leaves and tubers. The fungus can be spread from infected to healthy plants through wind, rain, dew, and high humidity. The disease causes most severe crop damage in moist, cool weather. Management Late blight can be controlled in different ways, before and after disease appearance. • Plant good quality, certified, seed tubers. Preferably save your own healthy seed tubers. • Cultivate resistant varieties • Locate your garden on well-drained soil where it will receive morning sun • Apply water around the base of plants and avoid wetting foliage in the early evening • Do not cultivate potato in infected fields for at least two years. Always practice appropriate crop rotation. • Remove blighted tubers before putting in store • Use fungicidal sprays on the appearance of initial symptoms, e.g. Dithane M-45 or chlorothalonil (Bravo) 500 SC, 2.5 kg/1 000 litres of water/hectare or Metiram (Polyram) 70 WG 1.8kg/ha, Propineb (Antracol) 70 WG 1.8kg/ha, Cymoxanil+ mancozeb, (Curzate M) 2.5kg/ha. Spraying should be repeated at 10-12 day intervals. Do not wash potatoes that are to be stored.
