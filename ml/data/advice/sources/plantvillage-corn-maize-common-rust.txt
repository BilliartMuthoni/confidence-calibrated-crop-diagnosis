Cause Fungus Comments Disease is spread by wind-borne spores; some of the most popularly grown sweet corn varieties have little or no resistance to the disease. Management The most effective method of controlling the disease is to plant resistant hybrids; application of appropriate fungicides may provide some degree on control and reduce disease severity; fungicides are most effective when the amount of secondary inoculum is still low, generally when plants only have a few rust pustules per leaf.
